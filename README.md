# Mathematics And AI

A selected list of papers, reports, articles and online resources that on the limits and problems with generative AI capacities and use in mathematics. It includes some software tools/projects. Only resources that have been read, or, in the case of software projects, evaluated, by the author, are included. It is hoped that this will be useful for people interested in this topic, because much of the discussion around AI in mathematics is publicity generated by the AI industry.

The items in the list are in no particular order, and will be updated over time.

1. Bastounis, A., Circelli, F. and Hansen, A.C. (2026) “Navier-Stokes lost in translation: Why Lean verification of AI autoformalisation does not guarantee correct natural language proofs.” arXiv. [doi:10.48550/ARXIV.2610.08144](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08144).

2. Association for Human Mathematics (AHM). Available at: [https://www.ahmath.org/home](https://www.ahmath.org/home) (Accessed: November 10, 2026).

3. Alper, J. et al. (2026) “Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics.” arXiv. [doi:10.48550/ARXIV.2609.35334](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.35334).

4. Nikankin, Y. et al. (2024) “Arithmetic Without Algorithms: Language Models Solve Math With a Bag of Heuristics.” arXiv. [doi:10.48550/ARXIV.2410.21272](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21272).

5. GitHub - 0xnurl/gpts-cant-count: Demo of even the most advanced LLMs inability to handle basic arithmetic. (no date) GitHub. Available at: https://github.com/0xnurl/gpts-cant-count (Accessed: November 7, 2026).

6. Why is it all in the kernel? (30 July 2026) lawrencecpaulson.github.io. Available at: https://lawrencecpaulson.github.io/2026/07/30/Collatz.html (Accessed: November 10, 2026). **[NOTE: This is about the very recent false AI-assisted disproof of the Collatz conjecture that relied on bugs in LEAN.]**

7. Antun, V., Colbrook, M.J. and Hansen, A.C. (2022) Proving Existence Is Not Enough: Mathematical Paradoxes Unravel the Limits of Neural Networks in Artificial Intelligence. Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM). Available at: https://www.siam.org/publications/siam-news/articles/proving-existence-is-not-enough-mathematical-paradoxes-unravel-the-limits-of-neural-networks-in-artificial-intelligence.
